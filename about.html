Our Story

In late 2018, our co-founder Aditya deposited ₹40,000 to an "ID provider" that a friend recommended. When he won ₹1.2 lakh on an IPL final, the site went cold. WhatsApp numbers blocked. Website down within a week. Four of his friends had similar experiences.

That's the Indian cricket ID market — or at least it was. Shady operators, no accountability, no content, terrible bonuses, and withdrawals that mysteriously fail the moment you actually win.

We started MyLeaseer in July 2019 with one rule: every single withdrawal gets paid, no matter the amount or the time of day. Seven years later, we've processed over ₹240 crore across 84,000+ withdrawal requests — with zero unpaid. Not one.

We're not the cheapest (our bonuses cost us), we're not the biggest (yet), and we're not the loudest on YouTube. But we're the one that pays, publishes predictions you can actually use, and treats users like customers instead of marks.
